Transition-path times of adenylate kinase, photon by photon
With M. Shehadeh, P. Manna, R. Ranguwar, A. Afanasyev, D. Makarov and G. Haran. Postdoctoral work, Weizmann Institute of Science.

How long an enzyme takes to cross the barrier between its open and closed conformations, measured in zero-mode waveguides with a photon-by-photon estimator validated against simulated ground truth. Further manuscripts on transition paths are under way.

Substrate-decoupled crowded lipid bilayers reveal configuration-dependent jamming and transport
S. Maurya, V. H. Rai, A. Upasani, S. Umrao, S. Nath, R. Roy. Doctoral work, Indian Institute of Science.

How a layer of grafted polymer, from sparse mushrooms to a dense brush, changes the mobility and assembly of molecules on a lipid membrane, once the drag of the supporting substrate is separated out.

A nucleation-limited pathway governs the lipid-modulated assembly of the pore-forming toxin Cytolysin A
With R. Roy and collaborators. Doctoral work, Indian Institute of Science.

Single-molecule photobleaching follows the size distribution of a toxin's oligomers as they assemble on a membrane in real time, the sequel to the 2018 PNAS study.
